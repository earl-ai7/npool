How to play

Click or drag on the table to aim the cue at a spot. Then grab the cue stick and pull it straight back; the farther you pull, the more power. Release to shoot. At the break and after a foul you have ball in hand: drag the white ball anywhere you like, as often as you like, until you shoot. Arrow keys fine-tune the aim (hold Shift for tiny steps) and Esc cancels a pull. Set the cue ball’s hit point first: high for follow, low for draw, left or right for English. Rules (standard 8-ball): the break is taken from behind the head string and must pocket a ball or drive four balls to a rail; otherwise your opponent may play on or make you re-break. The table stays open until a player legally pockets a ball. Every shot must hit your own group first (the 8 only once your group is gone) and then pocket a ball or drive a ball to a rail. A scratch, wrong first hit or no rail is a foul and gives your opponent the cue ball in hand. Sinking the 8 on the break re-racks. To win, call a pocket for the 8-ball (click it) and sink it there; sinking it early, in another pocket, or while fouling loses the game.
